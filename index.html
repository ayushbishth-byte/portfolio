About Ayush

Ayush Bisht is a photographer working across fashion, portraiture, and landscape photography. His work moves between the energy of the runway and the stillness of the mountains, guided throughout by a close attention to light, expression, and composition.

Photography, for Ayush, is a way of noticing — a discipline of paying attention to people and places long enough to see them clearly, and of finding images that feel both considered and alive.
